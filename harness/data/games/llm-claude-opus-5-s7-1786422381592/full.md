## 🎤 Debrief _(instrument v2; the model's own words, from its final transcript — a self-report, not ground truth)_

**1. How the game went**

A grindy, back-and-forth game. I built a full breaker suite early (Cleaver, Unity, Carmen) plus Pennyshaver and various drip economy, and took an early lead by stealing Orbital Superiority and Offworld Office out of remotes. The Corp fought back hard around turns 12–14: Public Trail tagged me, Retribution killed my Carmen, and a click-trash removed Red Team, which gutted both my rig and my economy simultaneously. I spent several turns rebuilding — a Mutual Favor off an Archives run reinstalled Carmen at a discount, and I clawed back to a Sure Gamble. Meanwhile the Corp scored Superconducting Hub and pulled to 3 points. On turn 15 I finally had money and a full rig, ran R&D through Palisade and Karunā with Tread Lightly, and hit Send a Message off the top of R&D for the winning 3 points.

**2. Plan and how it evolved**

Initial plan: build economy, get all three breakers, and pressure centrals (especially R&D) while contesting remotes when cheap. That worked until the Corp's tag package punished me — after Retribution and the Red Team trash I switched to pure rebuild mode: draw, bank credits, remove tags immediately (Orbital Superiority + a tag is 4 meat damage and I was often at 2–3 cards), and only run when I could break everything cleanly. The final phase was single-access R&D digging, which is low-percentage per run but was the only server I could reliably get into; it paid off immediately.

**3. Key turning points**

- Corp turn 13 (Public Trail → Retribution on Carmen → trash Red Team): the single worst swing, costing me a breaker, my best economy card, and effectively three turns of tempo.
- The Archives run on turn 13: worthless in accesses but it enabled Mutual Favor to fetch and install Carmen at a 2-credit discount — that's what made the winning R&D run possible two turns later.
- Turn 15: choosing Tread Lightly on R&D. The +3 rez tax made Palisade cost the Corp 6 credits, dropping them from 10 to 4, and I still had enough left (8c) to break both ice for 5 and breach.

**4. What I'd do differently**

I over-invested clicks in marginal Archives runs and let my grip get dangerously thin (2 cards) around the damage-heavy ice, which forced me to fully break Karunā every time rather than take a cheap hit. I also should have run the Remote 0 card that the Corp double-advanced on turn 14 rather than spending two clicks on 1-credit gains — I had no way in that turn, but it meant Superconducting Hub scored uncontested. More generally, I let my credit pool bottom out repeatedly; keeping a 5-credit floor would have let me punish remotes on the turn they appeared.

**5. Interface friction**

The main annoyance was the Archives breach: I had to click through ten separate "select which already-trashed card to access" decisions with zero gameplay content. A single "access all Archives cards" confirmation would be much better. Otherwise the state JSON was clear and complete — I especially appreciated advancement counters being shown on hidden cards, and the "then" field on command options which made it obvious when playing an event committed me to a specific server.
